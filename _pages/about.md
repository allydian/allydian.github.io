Hi there! I'm Ally and I believe in an accessibly sustainable future. I am a postgraduate candidate applying game-based learning and gamification in non-immersive and immersive learning environments to tackle community concerns. I earned my BSc in Computer Science with Honours from Universiti Malaysia Sarawak, and am currently in the process of earning my MSc there as well in the same field!

My interest lies in improving software usability for diverse communities, and using them as a medium for the preservation and dissemination of knowledge. Particularly, I am fascinated by the potential avenues they serve for cultural heritage preservation, even more so when they're presented as lore-heavy games. My works so far have been designed and developed with disabled, digitally hesitant, and educationally marginalised communities in mind. I ensure that the outcome is inclusive to all by placing their needs most and lived experiences at the forefront of the design process and co-creating with them.

My experience includes teaching undergraduate students as a lab demonstrator, and engaging with local urban and rural, and international communities as a research assistant. [Visit here for my CV](/cv/).
