In this colloquium, I presented the preprint version of "Evaluating Herbal Heritage as a knowledge transfer medium for gamified learning of Kenyah and Kayan traditional medicinal knowledge using extended reality" which was later published in Computer Animation and Virtual Worlds as "Evaluating the usability of Herbal Heritage as a knowledge transfer medium for gamified learning of Kenyah and Kayan traditional medicinal knowledge using extended reality". This paper had won the Best Paper Award.
