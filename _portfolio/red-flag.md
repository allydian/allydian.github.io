This was a solo development project that served as part of an undergraduate final year project. Everything (except for the audio assets!) was done by me under the guidance of my supervisor, the lovely Dr. Amelia Jati Robert Jupit. It was developed as a medium for teaching and evaluating primary schoolchildren's knowledge on child grooming. The findings of this research was reported in this <a href="https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11216642/">article</a>. Further information can be found <a href="https://allydian.itch.io/red-flag">here</a>.
