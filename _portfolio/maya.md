This was our team's entry for the ASWARA 48 Hour XR Jam. The concepts explored a museum-like centre with two rooms. Each of them featured a core heritage: (1) Sarawak cuisine workshop, and (2) Sarawak folklore. Illustrations in the Asset Concept sections were produced by me.
