This was developed as a group of 5 students as an undergraduate semester project. With the assignment calling for the Covid-19 pandemic as its theme, we decided to tell the stories of 3 affected Malaysian lives, all from different socioeconomic standings in society. Everything except for the audion assets were produced by us. This was developed using Unity, and was playable on Android.
