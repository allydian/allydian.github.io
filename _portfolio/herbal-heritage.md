This was co-developed with Nurul Farizah Ridzuan as part of our Masters research project. It incorporates gamficiation elements, AR, and non-immersive VR in an effort to preserve the Kenyah and Kayan cultural heritage that has kept their communities safe and healthy over the centuries. This was used in the final study, and the results were reported in a journal article available <a href="https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/cav.70167">here</a>.  The application itself can be installed from the <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.FarAlly.HeritageHealerWithAR&hl=en_IN">Google Playstore</a> only.
