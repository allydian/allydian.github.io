This VR demo was developed as the VR example used in a pilot study as a demonstration of how XR technologies can be utilised to digitally document and disseminate traditional medicinal knowledge. Particularly, it demonstrated the simulation of herbal medicine foraging, much like how our Bornean ancestors once did and their descendents still do for natural remedies. A preprint reporitng on the outcomes of the pilot study is available <a href="https://journal.uob.edu.bh/items/3e51528e-6f8e-4154-8362-5fd7b9d31478">here</a>. Herbal Heritage was developed based on the feedback obtained from this pilot study.
