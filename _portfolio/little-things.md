This was developed as our team entry at the Biji-Biji Initiatives' Games Bagus 2022. Its themed around mental health, particularly, depression. It utilises game mechanics such as fishing mechanics to simulate the effort required to keep the player's room livable. In turn, the player is rewarded with bits and pieces of their home returning to colour. The player is allowed to play at their own pace, enacting their autonomy for making dignifid choices. The entry can be played <a href="https://zphyrus.itch.io/little-things-demo">here</a>.
